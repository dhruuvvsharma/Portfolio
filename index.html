Black Friday Sales Prediction
Live

Problem

Retailers need to estimate how much a customer will spend during a high volume sale event, using only basic customer and product attributes, so pricing and inventory decisions aren't guesswork.

What it does

An end to end pipeline covering ingestion, feature engineering, model training and tuning across multiple models, landing on a tuned XGBoost regressor. It's containerized with Docker and served through a FastAPI backend and Streamlit UI, with a Power BI dashboard for the business side.

NOX: Voice AI Assistant
Live

Problem

Most personal assistants either need constant typing or feel clunky to talk to. NOX is my attempt at a voice first assistant that listens, understands intent and responds naturally.

What it does

Wakes up on a wake word and takes it from there. Under the hood it's an ML based intent classifier, not a plain if else rule ladder, with an LLM as a fallback for anything it doesn't recognize. It can open local apps like Spotify, Notepad, your browser and VS Code, search the web for you, play a song on YouTube on request, and pull specific facts from Wikipedia when you ask.
